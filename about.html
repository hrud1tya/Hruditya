About

Hi, I'm Hruditya Rajwade. I started out on production sets in Mumbai, across films and TV commercials. Later, I went from Disney intern to producer at Star Sports and JioStar. There, I delivered content strategy and production for 25+ tournaments that reached billions of fans across social, streaming and TV. I then moved to London for an MSc in Marketing at Queen Mary. Since then, I've worked with an elite sports performance consultancy on marketing strategy, partnerships and content, from ideation to execution. I've also delivered brand and sales activations for Remitly with England Cricket, and for Bigen and Dabur. I think like a marketer and execute like a producer, turning briefs into work that brings brands closer to their consumers.
